Terms of use

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Intended use

MailBridge is a private application for its owner's personal email review. These pages do not offer public registration, an app download, or a hosted service. Connect only accounts you own or are authorized to manage.

Review before acting

Analysis and generated replies can be incorrect or incomplete. Check the original message, dates, recipients, and proposed text before relying on an item or saving a draft. Attachments are not analyzed and linked pages are not opened.

MailBridge can add organizational labels automatically. Saving a Gmail draft requires an explicit request after review. Sending a message remains an action taken separately in Gmail; MailBridge does not send or delete mail.

Owner responsibilities

The owner manages the Windows host, access credentials, phone pairing, network access, backups, and data removal. Read the privacy policy before connecting an account, especially the disclosure about email content sent to hosted Claude analysis.

The owner must keep Claude's model-improvement setting off for this use and follow the applicable terms of Google, Anthropic, Apple, and Tailscale. MailBridge cannot enforce every setting or policy of those services.

Availability and limitations

Synchronization and notifications depend on the host running, network connectivity, account authorization, and external services. Delivery may be delayed or fail. MailBridge provides no guarantee of continuous availability, accurate classification, or timely reminders; check Gmail directly for time-sensitive matters.

Stopping use

The owner can stop the host and revoke Google access at any time. Revoking access or disconnecting the phone does not delete stored records. The privacy policy explains provider processing and manual local data removal.

For questions about the application, use the support email shown on MailBridge's Google authorization screen.
